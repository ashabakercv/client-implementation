# Harbor Learning launch plan

Personal demonstration using a fictional client. Target launch: 30 November 2026.

## Scope
Launch one initial cohort on a learning platform. Include account setup, a sample data check, role-based access verification, administrator orientation, user acceptance and a documented handoff. Exclude custom integrations, procurement and migration of historical records.

## Delivery sequence
| Gate | Owner | Evidence required | Dependency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scope confirmed | Implementation lead | Approved scope and named client owner | None |
| Client workspace ready | Platform administrator | Test accounts and role/access results | Scope confirmed |
| Sample data accepted | Client data owner | Validation results and resolved exceptions | Scope confirmed |
| UAT accepted | Client sponsor | Signed test results and accepted residual issues | Workspace and data ready |
| Training complete | Enablement lead | Administrator walkthrough and job aid | Workspace ready |
| Launch approved | Client sponsor | All required gates complete and critical issues closed | UAT and training |

## Working cadence
Collect owner updates before the weekly meeting. State what changed, what is due next, and what needs a decision. Discuss blocked work first. Publish decisions and assigned actions after the meeting. Escalate a critical issue immediately rather than waiting for the next status cycle.

## Change handling
A new request must name its outcome, affected deliverables and expected timing. The implementation lead assesses impact; the sponsor chooses whether to substitute scope, move the date or defer the request. Record the decision and communicate the updated baseline.
